🏋️ Station 9: Eye Gym (Bates method & friends)

🤓 Real talk from Dr. Kent: The Bates method is from 1920, and studies have not shown that it fixes nearsightedness, farsightedness or astigmatism. These exercises can feel relaxing and help with screen strain (the 20-20-20 rule is legit), but please keep your glasses for driving and flying. We skip Bates' "sunning", because staring at the sun damages your eyes.
